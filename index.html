Frequently asked

Do I need an account to see pricing?+

No. Every price on the site is visible without logging in. You'll only need a free account to check out.

What do I need for tax-exempt wholesale pricing?+

A current resale certificate and your business Tax ID. Once verified, tax-exempt case pricing and net terms apply automatically at checkout.

Are hemp-derived THC/CBD products legal to resell?+

It depends on your state — some restrict or ban hemp-derived THC products entirely. Check each product page and your local regulations before ordering; retailers are responsible for confirming their own compliance.

Is there a minimum order?+

No case minimums on standard orders. Tax-exempt wholesale accounts may see additional volume pricing at higher case counts.

Where do you ship from?+

Orders ship from three regional warehouses, typically arriving within 24–48 hours of dispatch for most of the continental US.
